Quickculator – Trade Smarter, Faster

A lightweight, purpose-built calculator for traders who don’t have time to waste. Quickulator handles margin, position size, and risk management with speed and clarity — no clutter, no distractions.

Traders can also create and save custom formulas, making repetitive calculations effortless. It’s designed to be fast, accurate, and always at hand, so users can focus on trading decisions instead of doing the math.

Entekhab-e Talaei – Better Mental Health Starts Here

Entekhabe Talaei was built for a child psychologist with a clear mission: to help parents discover their children’s unique talents and challenges. Instead of long clinic visits or stressful environments, the app allows parents to guide their children through recognized psychological tests at home — in a space where they feel safe and comfortable.

Once the results are submitted, they go directly to the psychologist, who carefully analyzes them and provides parents with a clear picture of their child’s strengths and areas for growth. Beyond testing, the app also enables parents to easily schedule follow-up appointments, making professional guidance more accessible than ever.
